Fourier Series

Periodic functions, coefficients, convergence

Definition

  • \[f(x) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (a_n\cos(nx) + b_n\sin(nx))\]
  • Period: \[2\pi\]

Coefficients

  • \[a_0 = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)dx\]
  • \[a_n = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\cos(nx)dx\]
  • \[b_n = \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x)\sin(nx)dx\]

Half-Range Series

  • Cosine Series: \[f(x) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n\cos(nx)\]
  • Sine Series: \[f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n\sin(nx)\]

Properties

  • Linearity: \[F\{af(x) + bg(x)\} = aF\{f(x)\} + bF\{g(x)\}\]
  • Parseval's Theorem: \[\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} |f(x)|^2dx = \frac{a_0^2}{4} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (a_n^2 + b_n^2)\]
