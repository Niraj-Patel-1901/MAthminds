Regression & Curve Fitting

Linear regression, correlation, least squares method

Linear Regression

  • Slope: \[b = \frac{n\sum xy - \sum x\sum y}{n\sum x^2 - (\sum x)^2}\]
  • Intercept: \[a = \bar{y} - b\bar{x}\]
  • Line equation: \[y = a + bx\]

Correlation

  • Pearson: \[r = \frac{n\sum xy - \sum x\sum y}{\sqrt{[n\sum x^2 - (\sum x)^2][n\sum y^2 - (\sum y)^2]}}\]
  • Spearman: \[r_s = 1 - \frac{6\sum d^2}{n(n^2-1)}\]
  • R-squared: \[R^2 = r^2\]
