Probability

Distributions, Bayes' theorem

Input

For Binomial: param1 = n, param2 = p.   For Poisson: param1 = λ (use param2/3 empty).   For Normal: param1 = μ, param2 = σ.

Solution

Enter distribution parameters and event to calculate probability.

Formula Sheet

Binomial: \[P(X=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k}\]
Poisson: \[P(X=k)=\frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}\]
Normal pdf: \[f(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}\]
Exponential pdf: \[f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\]
Bayes: \[P(A|B)=\frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}\]
Joint: \[P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\]
