Complex Numbers

D'Moivre's theorem, roots of unity, and polar form

Basic Operations

  • Addition: \[(a+bi) + (c+di) = (a+c) + (b+d)i\]
  • Multiplication: \[(a+bi)(c+di) = (ac-bd) + (ad+bc)i\]
  • Division: \[\frac{a+bi}{c+di} = \frac{(ac+bd) + (bc-ad)i}{c^2+d^2}\]

Polar Form

  • Conversion: \[z = r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta) = re^{i\theta}\]
  • Modulus: \[r = |z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\]
  • Argument: \[\theta = \arg(z) = \tan^{-1}(\frac{b}{a})\]

D'Moivre's Theorem

  • \[(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta) + i\sin(n\theta)\]
  • \[(re^{i\theta})^n = r^ne^{in\theta}\]

Roots of Unity

  • nth roots: \[z^{\frac{1}{n}} = r^{\frac{1}{n}}(\cos\frac{\theta + 2\pi k}{n} + i\sin\frac{\theta + 2\pi k}{n})\]
  • where \[k = 0,1,2,...,n-1\]
