Eigenvalues & Multiplicities
Characteristic equation: \[ \det(\lambda I - A) = 0 \] - Algebraic multiplicity: exponent of root in characteristic polynomial.
- Geometric multiplicity: dimension of eigenspace.
- A matrix is diagonalizable ⇔ for each eigenvalue, algebraic multiplicity = geometric multiplicity.
Eigenvectors
For eigenvalue \(\lambda\), eigenvectors satisfy: \[ (A - \lambda I)\mathbf{x} = 0 \]
Diagonalization
If \(A\) has \(n\) linearly independent eigenvectors, then \[ A = PDP^{-1}, \quad D = \text{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n) \]
Cayley–Hamilton
Every square matrix satisfies its own characteristic polynomial: \[ p(\lambda) = \det(\lambda I - A), \quad p(A) = 0 \]
Similarity of Matrices
Matrices \(A\) and \(B\) are similar if there exists an invertible matrix \(P\) such that \[ A = PBP^{-1} \] Similar matrices share the same determinant, trace, eigenvalues, and characteristic polynomial.
