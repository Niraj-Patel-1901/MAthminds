Double/Triple Integrals

Multiple integration, change of variables

Double Integrals

  • \[\iint_R f(x,y)dxdy\]
  • \[\int_a^b\int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)} f(x,y)dydx\]

Triple Integrals

  • \[\iiint_V f(x,y,z)dxdydz\]
  • \[\int_a^b\int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)}\int_{h_1(x,y)}^{h_2(x,y)} f(x,y,z)dzdydx\]

Polar Coordinates

  • \[\iint_R f(x,y)dxdy = \iint_R f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)rdrd\theta\]
  • \[x = r\cos\theta, y = r\sin\theta\]

Spherical Coordinates

  • \[\iiint_V f(x,y,z)dxdydz = \iiint_V f(\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\rho\cos\phi)\rho^2\sin\phi d\rho d\phi d\theta\]
  • \[x = \rho\sin\phi\cos\theta, y = \rho\sin\phi\sin\theta, z = \rho\cos\phi\]
