Partial Differentiation

Chain rule, total differential, Euler's theorem and more

Partial Derivatives

  • \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x},\ \frac{\partial f}{\partial y},\ \frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\)
  • \(\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2},\ \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y},\ \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x}\)
  • Clairaut's theorem: \(\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x}\)

Chain Rule

  • Single var: \(\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{\partial y}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{du}{dx}\)
  • Multi var: \(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial u} \cdot \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v} \cdot \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\)
  • General: \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = \sum_i \frac{\partial f}{\partial u_i} \cdot \frac{\partial u_i}{\partial x}\)

Total Differential

  • Two vars: \(df = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}dx + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}dy\)
  • Three vars: \(df = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}dx + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}dy + \frac{\partial f}{\partial z}dz\)

Euler’s Theorem

  • Two vars: \(x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} + y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = n f\)
  • Three vars: \(x\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} + y\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} + z\frac{\partial f}{\partial z} = n f\)
  • Deductions: \(x^2 f_{xx} + 2xy f_{xy} + y^2 f_{yy} = n(n-1)f\)

Coordinate Transformations

  • Polar: \(x = r\cos\theta,\ y = r\sin\theta\)
  • Cylindrical: \(x = r\cos\theta,\ y = r\sin\theta,\ z=z\)
  • Spherical: \(x = \rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\ y = \rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\ z = \rho\cos\phi\)
