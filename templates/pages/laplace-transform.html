Laplace Transform

Properties, applications, inverse transform

Definition

  • \[F(s) = \int_0^\infty f(t)e^{-st}dt\]
  • Inverse: \[f(t) = \frac{1}{2\pi j} \int_{c-j\infty}^{c+j\infty} F(s)e^{st}ds\]

Properties

  • Linearity: \[L\{af(t) + bg(t)\} = aF(s) + bG(s)\]
  • Time Shift: \[L\{f(t-a)u(t-a)\} = e^{-as}F(s)\]
  • Frequency Shift: \[L\{e^{at}f(t)\} = F(s-a)\]

Common Pairs

  • \[1 \to \frac{1}{s}\]
  • \[e^{at} \to \frac{1}{s-a}\]
  • \[\sin(at) \to \frac{a}{s^2+a^2}\]
  • \[\cos(at) \to \frac{s}{s^2+a^2}\]

Differential Equations

  • \[L\{f'(t)\} = sF(s) - f(0)\]
  • \[L\{f''(t)\} = s^2F(s) - sf(0) - f'(0)\]
