Non-Linear Programming

Kuhn-Tucker, penalty methods

Kuhn-Tucker Conditions

  • \[\nabla f(x^*) + \sum \lambda_i \nabla g_i(x^*) = 0\]
  • \[\lambda_i g_i(x^*) = 0\]
  • \[\lambda_i \geq 0\]

Penalty Method

  • \[P(x,r) = f(x) + r \sum [g_i(x)]^2\]
  • \[r \to \infty\]

Quadratic Programming

  • \[\min \frac{1}{2}x^TQx + c^Tx\]
  • \[Ax \leq b\]

Methods

  • Interior Point
  • Sequential Quadratic
  • Barrier Method
  • Augmented Lagrangian
