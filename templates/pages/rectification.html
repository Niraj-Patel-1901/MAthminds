Rectification

Arc length, curve length

Cartesian Form

  • \[L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + (\frac{dy}{dx})^2}dx\]
  • \[L = \int_c^d \sqrt{1 + (\frac{dx}{dy})^2}dy\]

Parametric Form

  • \[L = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \sqrt{(\frac{dx}{dt})^2 + (\frac{dy}{dt})^2}dt\]
  • \[x = f(t), y = g(t)\]

Polar Form

  • \[L = \int_{\theta_1}^{\theta_2} \sqrt{r^2 + (\frac{dr}{d\theta})^2}d\theta\]
  • \[r = f(\theta)\]

Space Curve

  • \[L = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \sqrt{(\frac{dx}{dt})^2 + (\frac{dy}{dt})^2 + (\frac{dz}{dt})^2}dt\]
  • \[x = f(t), y = g(t), z = h(t)\]
